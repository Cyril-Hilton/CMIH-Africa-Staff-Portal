# Operations dashboard upgrade and weekly reporting access

## Implemented

1. The weekly reporting region is visible on the default super admin/executive dashboard. Users can choose all six departments or All Departments without first constructing a department URL. Existing executive views and departmental permission checks are preserved.
2. William Quartey (live-verified Brands HOD account 18) joins automatic attendance and the daily task **Coaching, directing and driving teams accountability**, effective 27 November 2026. No earlier days are backfilled for William. Sessions remain open before 18:00 Accra and close at 18:00 after that time. Weekends, registered non-working dates and approved leave do not generate automatic work. The grant requires his active Brands manager profile; it does not apply to William Hamilton Junior in Operations.
3. Master projects, Suppliers and Production & execution are removed from Operations dashboard tables and entry controls. Their historical stored records remain intact. Old Add/Edit links for retired kinds are handled without breaking the page, and new submissions for those kinds are rejected.
4. Each current Operations report register has independent search, column/value filter, sort column, direction and page-size controls. These apply to the entire matching query before pagination, with numeric sorting for numeric fields and staff-name matching for owners. State persists across neighbouring table controls and pagination. Original source rows, overview breakdowns and issue/return history also have dedicated controls. Related detail tables retain local table controls.
5. Shared asset filtering now includes explicit sort controls for item, brand, current/warehouse location, type/category, condition, status and assigned person. Dedicated asset sorting takes priority over legacy defaults. The order is respected in the Operations inventory, warehouse manager, office manager and HR asset register. Existing brand/location/handler/project/department constraints combine before pagination.
6. Activation Excel/CSV import has a downloadable blank template, configurable date interpretation, workbook preview, unknown-column acknowledgement and a separate submit step. Every row enters the existing pending Operations submission workflow, including HOD imports. No imported activation contributes to published dashboard totals until HOD approval. Source workbook fingerprint prevents repeated submission from duplicating rows; invalid workbooks fail without partial submission. Staff imports use their own owner; HOD imports may assign active Operations owners. The first worksheet is read, values-only .xlsx and CSV are supported, with 500 rows / 10 MB upload limits and bounded worksheet expansion. Excel serial dates, including 1904 workbooks, are interpreted explicitly.
7. Activation exports support Excel and PDF, selectable columns, current period/staff/search/table-filter scope, and a maximum 2,000 rows. Requesting an export freezes the exact approved-data snapshot. HOD approval is required before the requester can download it; non-manager staff cannot approve, and unrelated staff cannot inspect or download another person's request. Later dashboard edits do not silently change approved file content. Review and download events are audited and notifications remain in-app.
8. Four distinct overview diagrams render without a remote chart dependency: execution trend line/area, current activation-status donut, activations-by-brand bars and reported POSM deployment/return flow. Totals use approved period records and page filters. Missing or invalid POSM quantities are called out and excluded rather than presented as completed returns. The POSM flow is labelled as reporting data, not warehouse stock. Individual table filters deliberately affect only that table; page filters control the diagrams.
