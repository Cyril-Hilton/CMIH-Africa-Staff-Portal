## End to end source connections

- Approved independent Operations activations and direct Creative briefs appear in the portfolio without requiring a fabricated Client Relations project. Execution compliance/staffing requirements apply to execution work, not every Creative-only brief.
- Operations staff enter schedule, required/confirmed staffing, crew/vendor bookings, external assets, compliance and execution tasks in the original activation entry. Pending proposals do not publish controls, tasks or warehouse requests. HOD publication updates the linked records once and preserves original entrant and publisher provenance. A stale revision cannot silently replace a newer plan.
- Client Relations process milestones use the original client brief receipt date and manager-reviewed publication. Both source owners and participants can open the shared plan; they do not gain executive editing access.
- Warehouse catalog quantities, requests, custodian, issue/return and movement history remain original sources. Publishing a plan does not consume stock. Overcommitted approved requests are flagged instead of earning full readiness.
- Existing exact task links connect original budgets, supplier invoice and claim processing states. They do not create extra revenue, cash or expense entries. Original upstream/downstream dependencies expose the awaited owner and clear after upstream verification.
- Approved absence windows give coverage/reassignment context without medical reasons or automatic delivery credit. Afternoon pass-out does not block morning work.
- Staffing achievement reuses the approved activation plan: 9 confirmed against 10 required earns 90%. Unknown plans earn zero; an explicit zero-staff requirement is excluded.
