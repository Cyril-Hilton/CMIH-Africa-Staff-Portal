# Assigned-work warnings and dashboard placement

Personal KPI expectations now come from owned/assigned work or explicit KPI definitions and plans, rather than every empty departmental category. Unassigned work remains unscored and does not receive invented performance credit. Missing results and recorded shortfalls require a due reporting obligation. Future tasks do not produce incomplete-delivery warnings. Submitted work awaiting manager action remains visible in its review queue without creating a personal submitter warning. Manager overdue reviews, genuine overdue work and attendance outcomes remain tracked. Missing manager quality grades are not staff failures.

The shared KPI Achievement & Reporting Gaps cards now follow each department's reporting workspace, retaining department colours and collapsible details. Operations Milestones and Campaigns tables are hidden from its dashboard; records, routes and project history remain intact.
